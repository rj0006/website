Who We Are

Your dependable transport partner

Om Trans India is a fleet owner and transport contractor based in Poodur Village, Medchal, Hyderabad. We provide trucks and containers of every common size — 14 ft, 17 ft, 20 ft, 22 ft, 32 ft SXL and 32 ft MXL — for transport all over India.

Because we run our own fleet, we control the condition of every vehicle and the quality of every trip. Whether you need one truck today or a regular vehicle for your factory, we make transport simple.
